# Release Notes

## Observability Dashboard

**Release Date:** January 10, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

### Description

The Observability Dashboard is a new feature within our DevOps platform designed to provide centralized, real-time visibility into the health and performance of your applications and infrastructure. By consolidating metrics, logs, and traces into a unified view, DevOps engineers can detect, investigate, and resolve issues more efficiently than ever before. This tool is integral for both proactive monitoring and reactive troubleshooting, enhancing your ability to maintain robust and reliable systems.

### Use Cases

- **Centralized Monitoring:** DevOps engineers can now view all service metrics, logs, and traces in one place, significantly reducing the need to switch between multiple disjointed tools.
- **Incident Response:** Quickly identify root causes of incidents by correlating alerts with relevant metrics and logs, streamlining the troubleshooting process.
- **Performance Analysis:** Utilize time-series graphs to track and compare performance metrics over time, enabling better-informed optimization efforts.
- **Customizable Views:** Tailor the dashboard to display critical information pertinent to your team’s specific needs, prioritizing essential services and environments.

### Usage Instructions

#### Accessing the Dashboard

1. **Navigate to the Observability Section:** Within the DevOps platform, locate and click on the 'Observability' tab to access the dashboard.
2. **Configure Your View:**
   - Use the drag-and-drop functionality to arrange widgets according to your preferences.
   - Customize data refresh intervals between 10 seconds to 5 minutes based on your monitoring needs.
   - Save and share your dashboard configurations with team members for collaborative monitoring.

#### Viewing Metrics

- Utilize the metrics panel to access visualizations of CPU, memory, disk I/O, and network usage.
- Input PromQL queries for advanced metrics analysis and create custom queries compatible with Prometheus.

#### Searching Logs

- Connect with external logging systems like Elasticsearch or Loki to view logs.
- Use the search and filter options to isolate logs by keyword, pod/service ID, or timestamp.

#### Tracing Requests

- Visualize distributed traces using integrated OpenTelemetry data.
- Drill down into specific services and inspect trace details such as duration, error rates, and individual spans.

#### Managing Alerts

- Monitor active alerts along with their severity levels directly from the dashboard.
- Quickly navigate to related metrics, logs, or traces to expedite issue resolution.
- Acknowledge or suppress alerts as needed to manage notification overload.
