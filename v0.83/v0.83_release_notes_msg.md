@channel Facets Release vX.XX is live! 🎉  
Here are the key updates:

**What’s New**
- **Observability Dashboard**: Gain real-time, centralized visibility into application and infrastructure health. This new feature consolidates metrics, logs, and traces into a single view, streamlining operations and enhancing incident response times.
  - **Unified Monitoring**: View all service metrics, logs, and traces in one centralized dashboard.
  - **Root Cause Analysis**: Quickly drill down into logs and traces to identify the root cause of issues.
  - **Custom Dashboards**: Customize dashboard layouts to prioritize critical services or environments.

**Workflows**
- **Operational Task Automation**: Execute operational workflows like pod restarts, database migrations, and cluster scaling directly from Terraform modules.
  - **Enhanced Resource Management**: Trigger and monitor workflows through the Facets UI or API.
  - **Monitoring and Logging**: Comprehensive monitoring and logging of workflow executions for detailed operational insights.
