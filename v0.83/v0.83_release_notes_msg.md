@channel Facets Release vX.XX is live! 🎉  
Here are the key updates:

**What’s New**
- **Observability Dashboard**: Gain a unified, real-time view of your application and infrastructure health. This feature consolidates metrics, logs, and traces, enhancing your ability to detect, investigate, and resolve issues swiftly.
- **Workflows**: Automate operational tasks like pod restarts and database migrations directly within Terraform modules. The integration of utility modules into Tekton Tasks simplifies task management and execution.

**Enhancements**
- **Customizable Dashboards**: Tailor your monitoring view to prioritize critical metrics and services, improving visibility and response times.
- **Enhanced Module Functionality**: Extend the capabilities of Terraform modules to include post-provisioning operations, offering greater flexibility and control.

**Bug Fixes**
- Improved dollar referencing and variable mapping in the UI for a more seamless configuration experience.
